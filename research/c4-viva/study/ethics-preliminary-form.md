**Project:** J26-IT-360, AdaptLearn, Component 4: Intelligent Viva System.
**Researcher:** R. J. Vanderheyden (IT23212022). **Supervisor:** Natalie [surname and title].

**Aim:** To identify which speech fluency measures (pauses, speaking speed, filler words)
indicate communication difficulty when Sri Lankan undergraduates answer technical viva questions
in English, their second language, and to derive thresholds for an intelligent viva system.

**Participants:** About 10 to 12 SLIIT IT undergraduates aged 18 or over who have completed the
Programming Fundamentals module, invited directly by the researcher. About half report finding it hard to
explain answers in English, and about half report being comfortable. Participation is voluntary
and unrelated to any assessment.

**Procedure:** An online form (consent, background questions, three self-report statements and
a 10-question multiple-choice quiz, about 15 minutes), then a recorded practice viva in an online call (Zoom or Google Meet)
(about 15 minutes, five programming questions read from a fixed script, one observer
present). Only students who score at least 7 of 10 on the quiz are invited to the viva. Only the student's voice is analysed. No video is analysed.

**Risks:** Minimal. A student may feel mild discomfort speaking in a viva setting. There are no
course marks (the quiz score is used only to check eligibility), the questions are standard course material, and the student may stop at any time.

**Benefits:** Practice with viva-style questions; contribution to tools that support students
who struggle to explain technical answers in English.

**Data handling:** Participants are identified only by codes (P01 to P12). Recordings and
results are stored on the researcher's password-protected laptop and an offline backup drive,
not in shared or public storage. Audio may be transcribed by cloud services (Groq, Google
Colab); participants are told this before consenting. Data is deleted at the end of the
project (June 2027). Reports contain only aggregated results.

**Consent:** Written consent through the online form before any recording, with information
about recording, storage, cloud processing and withdrawal.

**Withdrawal:** Participants may stop the viva at any time and withdraw their data until it is
analysed, without giving a reason; their recording and results are then deleted.
