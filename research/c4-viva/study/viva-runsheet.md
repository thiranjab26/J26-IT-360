# Viva run sheet (one session, about 30 minutes)

Use this for the practice viva (code **P00**) and for every real session (P01 to P10). Keep it open
next to the script.

## What you need

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Laptop on charger, Zoom desktop app, you are the **host** | Only the host can record on the computer |
| Headphones with a mic (you, student, observer) | Stops your voice leaking into the student's track |
| `viva-script.md` (printed, or open in preview) | You read the questions word for word |
| `viva-cards.md` (open) | Copy each written example into the Zoom chat |
| `observer-form.md` (printed or digital, for the observer) | Observer ratings per question |
| Paper and pen | Note which follow-up you asked (none / B / C) per question |
| A clock or phone timer | For the 20-second "Take your time" rule |
| Phone on silent, notifications off | No noises or interruptions on the recording |

## 30 minutes before

1. Zoom settings (once): Recording, "Record a separate audio file for each participant" ON;
   Audio, "Original sound for musicians" ON.
2. Check free disk space (at least 2 GB).
3. Send the Zoom link to the student and the observer, with the "Tell each student" lines from
   `zoom-setup.md` (headphones, quiet room, Original sound).
4. Brief the observer (message or call): mic **muted** and camera off for the whole viva; never
   speak; fill one row per question; do not ask the student's group.

## 10 minutes before

1. Start the meeting. Headphones on. Script, cards and paper ready.
2. Click **Original sound** (top left) so it shows "on".

## When they join (not recorded)

1. Ask the student and the observer to rename themselves in Zoom (Participants, More, Rename):
   student **P00** (or their code), observer **Observer**. Zoom names each audio file after the
   display name, so you can tell the files apart and the student's name never appears.
2. Ask the student to click **Original sound** and confirm they have headphones on.
3. Sound check: ask the student to say one sentence; check it is clear.
4. Read the **Opening** from the script. Wait for "yes".

## Recording

1. Click **Record**, then **Record on this computer**. Check the red "Recording" label.
2. Say "Participant P00".
3. **Warm-up** (always do it, every student, so all sessions match): "Tell me briefly about your
   degree and why you chose it."
4. Questions 1 to 5, in order, word for word:
   - Paste the question's card into the chat first. Then say, in one go without stopping:
     "The first question is: ..." (later "Thank you. The next question is: ...") and the
     question word for word. Same words for every student; no "okay, good" or "nice" (they
     sound like feedback). For a follow-up with a card (Q1 B, Q2 C, Q4 C), paste that card too;
     a follow-up is not a new question, so say "Thank you." and the follow-up only.
   - Stay silent while the student thinks. No "mm-hm", no nodding sounds.
   - After about 20 seconds of silence: "Take your time." Nothing else.
   - Complete answer: "Thank you", next question.
   - Partly right: Follow-up B. Wrong or "I don't know": Follow-up C. Only one follow-up, then
     "Thank you" and move on.
   - Never say "good", "correct" or give a hint. Say "Thank you" after every answer, right or
     wrong, also before a follow-up ("Thank you. What happens if...").
   - Write on paper: Q1 none/B/C, Q2 none/B/C, and so on.
5. Do not talk while the student is talking. If they talk over you, stop, let them finish.
   While a student answers, say only "Take your time". Anything longer splits the answer in two
   in the analysis. A new question always comes right after "Thank you".
   Never pause the recording: reread the question instead.
6. Read the **Closing**: "That is the end of the viva. Thank you for taking part."
7. Click **Stop recording**.

## After recording (not recorded)

1. Optional, ask aloud and write down: "I knew the answers to most questions, 1 to 5" and
   "Speaking in English made it harder to explain, 1 to 5".
2. Thank them; tell the observer to stay a minute.
3. Ask the observer for the overall answer (Yes / No / Unsure) and get the form (photo is fine).
4. **End the meeting for all.** Zoom now converts the files; wait until it finishes.

## Files

1. Zoom opens the folder (or Documents, Zoom, the meeting folder). Open **Audio Record**: one
   `.m4a` per person, named after the display names.
2. Rename: the student's file to `P00_student.m4a`, yours to `P00_examiner.m4a`. Leave the
   observer's file out (delete it).
3. Move both to `Desktop/Research Project/Research Data/Recordings/`.
4. Copy the folder to the USB drive. Never upload it to Drive, GitHub or WhatsApp.
5. Real students only: fill their row in `Research Data/Participants/participants.csv`
   (observer column, notes, which follow-ups you asked).
6. Tell Claude "practice files are in" (P00) or "sessions done" (real vivas).

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Student has no headphones | Reschedule; their track would contain your voice |
| Recording did not start | Restart from the warm-up; say "Participant P00, restart" |
| Student drops out of the call | Pause; when they rejoin, repeat the current question |
| Only one audio file appears | The separate-audio setting was off; turn it on; redo the practice |
| Loud noise during an answer | Carry on; write the question number down |
