# Zoom set-up and file handling

## Once, on your laptop (Zoom desktop app)

1. Settings, **Recording**: turn on **"Record a separate audio file for each participant"**.
   Free accounts can do this with local recording ("Record on this computer").
2. Settings, **Audio**: under Audio Profile, choose **"Original sound for musicians"**. This stops
   Zoom's noise suppression removing quiet "um"s and breaths.
3. Use earphones. With original sound, echo cancellation is off, so your voice must not reach
   your microphone from the speakers.

## Tell each student (send with the Zoom link)

- Join from a laptop with **earphones**, in a quiet room, with a stable connection.
- In Zoom: Settings, Audio, Audio Profile, "Original sound for musicians". In the meeting, click
  the **Original sound** button at the top left (it turns off again for every new meeting).
- Keep the camera on if you are comfortable; only your voice is analysed.

## Each session

1. Check the student and the observer have joined and turned on Original sound.
2. Click **Record**, choose **Record on this computer**, then say "Participant P03".
3. Follow `viva-script.md`. Free Zoom meetings end after 40 minutes; one viva fits easily.
4. Stop the recording; end the meeting. Zoom converts the files when the meeting ends.

## After the session

1. Open Documents, Zoom, the meeting folder, **Audio Record**. There is one `.m4a` per person.
2. Rename them: `P03_student.m4a` and `P03_examiner.m4a` (the examiner file is your voice).
3. Move both into one folder for the study, for example `Documents/viva-study/recordings`.
   Back it up to a USB drive. Never upload it to GitHub, Google Drive or WhatsApp.
4. Fill in `participants.csv` (group, MCQ score, observer's overall answer).
