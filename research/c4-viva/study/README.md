# Study materials (PP1 pilot)

Use them in this order. All are drafts: adjust names and dates, and ask your supervisor or a
lecturer to check the questions.

| Step | File | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `ethics-preliminary-form.md` | Answers to paste into SLIIT's Preliminary Ethics Review Form |
| 2 | `google-form.md` | Consent, background, the three statements, the quiz, the self-rating |
| 3 | `mcq.md` | The 10 quiz questions with the answer key (pass mark 6 of 10) |
| 4 | `zoom-setup.md` | Zoom settings once, what to tell students, how to save and name files |
| 5 | `viva-script.md` | What you say in every viva, word for word, and the follow-up rule |
| 6 | `observer-form.md` | Printable form for the observer, one per student |
| 7 | `participants.template.csv` | Copy it to `Documents/viva-study/participants.csv` (outside the repository) and fill it in |

`participants.csv` columns: `code` (P01...), `group` (`difficulty` or `comfortable`),
`mcq_score` (0 to 100), `self_report` (average of the three statements), `observer` (yes, no,
unsure), `notes`.
